# Changelog

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-17

The first version.

- Add links by pasting anywhere, typing, dragging from a browser, a `lovelydl://` link, or
  the bookmarklet. Optionally, the app notices a link you just copied.
- A preview with title, length, size and the exact resolution before anything is saved.
- Presets: Best, Compatible MP4, 2160p to 480p caps, and M4A, MP3, Opus or FLAC audio; an
  Advanced format picker.
- A queue that survives restarts: limits per app and per site, pause, resume, cancel, retry,
  drag to reorder, partial downloads that resume.
- Playlists and channels with a selection sheet, their own folder and index numbers.
- Subtitles, embedded thumbnail, metadata and chapters, SponsorBlock, time ranges, live
  streams, rate limit, proxy, browser cookies, and a file name template with a live preview.
- Searchable history that notices moved or deleted files.
- yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno installed, verified, updated and rolled back by the app.
- Taskbar progress and badge, notifications, an optional tray icon, keep-awake, drag-out,
  light, dark and contrast themes, Reduce Motion.
- Windows caption buttons at the top right and the app's name at the top left; press
  feedback and eased hover states throughout.
- Waiting downloads show their titles and thumbnails before their turn.
- Downloads that drop out start themselves again, three times, waiting longer each time.
- Four fragments at a time by default, which is much faster on most sites.
- A line under the sidebar sections showing how many downloads run and how fast.
