# Code of conduct

lovelyDL follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be kind and assume good intent. People here give their time for free.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Keep it on topic. No spam, advertising, or requests to get around DRM or paywalls.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's personal information.

Report a problem to the maintainer through GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/FlyShip09/lovelyDL/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting @FlyShip09 directly. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people
who keep breaking these rules.
